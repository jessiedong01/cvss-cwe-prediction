\section{Conclusion}
A 3.8B-parameter model fine-tuned for \nLoraTrainHours{} hours predicts every NVD enrichment field more accurately than strong TF-IDF baselines on a full year of later CVEs, although the margins are modest and TF-IDF remains a fast and strong baseline that any new method should exceed.
Computing the score from the predicted vector keeps outputs consistent and helps a linear model, but it shifts errors toward over-calling \textsc{Critical}, a trade-off that users should choose deliberately, and the rarest severity tier, \textsc{Low}, remains poorly predicted by every system.
Natural next steps are rebalancing the training data, a second training epoch, larger open models, and evaluation on CVSS v4.0.
