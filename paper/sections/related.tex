\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{CVSS vector prediction.}
\citet{elbaz2020fighting} predicted each CVSS metric from a bag-of-words description with linear models and derived the score from the predicted vector, with an emphasis on explainable decisions at disclosure time.
CVSS-BERT~\citep{shahid2021cvssbert} trained one BERT classifier per CVSS v3.1 metric and computed the score with the specification's equations.
\citet{marchiori2025canllms} compared general-purpose LLMs with embedding-based classifiers for computing CVSS vectors and found the embedding methods stronger on the more subjective impact metrics (confidentiality, integrity, availability).
We follow the predict-the-vector design of these works and add a fine-tuned small language model, a strict temporal split, and a direct comparison with score regression.

\paragraph{Severity and CWE classification.}
VLAI~\citep{bonhomme2025vlai} fine-tunes RoBERTa on more than 600,000 vulnerabilities to predict the severity tier.
Its training set spans CVEs from many years, including 2024, so it is not comparable on our test set. We discuss this in Section~\ref{sec:data}.
For CWE assignment, \citet{aota2020automation} used bag-of-words features with random forests, and \citet{mosievskiy2026roberta} reports that a 125M-parameter RoBERTa is competitive with much larger LLMs.

\paragraph{LLMs for vulnerability triage.}
\citet{talibzade2025llms} found that TF-IDF classifiers beat general-purpose LLMs with generic prompts on CWE and severity assignment, and attribute this to the schematic wording of CVE descriptions.
CVE-LLM~\citep{ghosh2025cvellm} fine-tunes an LLM with a domain ontology to evaluate medical-device vulnerabilities.
\citet{torkamani2025casey} use LLMs to assign CWEs and severity in a triage tool.
For a related task, predicting exploitation, \citet{roytman2025benchmarking} reports that frontier LLMs fall well short of the statistical EPSS model and cost far more to run at scale.
Industry systems such as VulnWatch~\citep{databricks2025vulnwatch} prioritize vulnerabilities with LLMs on hosted infrastructure.
Our setting differs in two ways: everything runs locally, and every output is evaluated on vulnerabilities disclosed after the training data.
