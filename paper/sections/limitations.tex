\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Label noise.}
NVD vectors are treated as ground truth even though analysts disagree, NVD revises its records, and the organizations that report CVEs sometimes score them differently, so some of the reported errors are disagreements that a human reviewer would accept, and the data snapshot is pinned so that the reported numbers stay fixed as the labels change.

\paragraph{Single split and seed.}
Evaluation covers a single test year and each model is trained once, so the bootstrap intervals capture the sampling of test CVEs and leave variation from training unmeasured.
Only one 3.8B model is fine-tuned, for a single epoch, and its validation accuracy was still rising at the end of training (Figure~\ref{fig:val}), so larger models or longer training may perform better, and neither large hosted models nor a rebalanced training set for the fine-tuned model were evaluated.

\paragraph{CVSS version.}
New CVEs increasingly carry CVSS v4.0 vectors, and although the formula approach carries over because v4.0 also computes the score from the vector, it remains untested on v4.0 data.

\paragraph{Zero-shot sample.}
The zero-shot model is evaluated on \nSub{} random test CVEs drawn from the full test set, and the confidence intervals in Table~\ref{tab:subset} reflect the smaller sample.

\paragraph{Input length.}
Only English descriptions are used, and descriptions are cut at 2,000 characters, which affects \nTestClipped\% of test CVEs.
