\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Label noise.}
We treat NVD's vector as ground truth, but analysts disagree, NVD revises records, and the organizations that report CVEs sometimes score them differently.
Some of our ``errors'' are disagreements a human reviewer would accept.
We pin the data snapshot so that our numbers stay fixed even as the labels change.

\paragraph{Single split and seed.}
We test on a single year and train each model once.
The bootstrap intervals cover sampling of test CVEs, not variation from training.
We fine-tune one 3.8B model for one epoch, and validation accuracy was still rising at the end (Figure~\ref{fig:val}). Larger models or longer training may do better.
We did not run large hosted models, and we did not rebalance the training data for the fine-tuned model.

\paragraph{CVSS version.}
New CVEs increasingly carry CVSS v4.0 vectors.
The formula approach carries over, since v4.0 also computes the score from the vector, but we did not test it.

\paragraph{Zero-shot sample.}
We evaluate the zero-shot model on \nSub{} random test CVEs rather than all of them.
The confidence intervals in Table~\ref{tab:subset} reflect this.

\paragraph{Input length.}
We use English descriptions and cut descriptions at 2,000 characters, which affects \nTestClipped\% of test CVEs.
