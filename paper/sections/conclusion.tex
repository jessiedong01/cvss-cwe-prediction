\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Our results suggest that CVE enrichment is tractable with modest computational resources, using only CVE description text.
Our TF-IDF logistic regression model trains in just a few minutes, requires no GPU, and trails the fine-tuned LLM by just a few points in each CVSS component and CWE.
Moreover, our simple formula scoring, which costs nothing, rescues the TF-IDF model's rare tiers; we therefore recommend deploying formula scoring on top of TF-IDF classification in all but applications that prize precision over recall on Critical vulnerabilities.
The 3.8B fine-tuned model trains in just \nLoraTrainHours{} hours, and achieves better results across the board: more consistent severity scores and CVSS vectors, and CWEs outside the frequent CWEs.
At inference time, it takes on the order of a half second to predict each CVE.
If either throughput or accuracy were insufficient, we might train for more than one epoch (validation accuracy was still climbing; see Table~\ref{tab:ckpt}), oversample or weight the loss of Low predictions (class-balanced weights did help our TF-IDF model), or try larger open models amenable to fine-tuning.
We leave these directions to future work.

We did not evaluate CVSS v4.0 vectors, which are starting to appear for new CVEs.
We expect formula scoring to work with these vectors too, though we have not tested it.
Our fine-tuned LLM could perhaps be retrained on the new format.

Our zero-shot LLM performs poorly by comparison.
We do not recommend using it for CVE enrichment.

Practitioners might wonder how best to use these models.
While we did not train or evaluate an ensemble, both our trained models make errors on mostly different CVEs; in principle, then, they might achieve up to \nEitherSev\% accuracy on tiers if ensembled somehow (e.g., by prediction agreement or model confidence).
An ensemble along these lines might or might not come close to this upper bound; we leave such investigations to future work.
Until then, we would be loath to deploy either trained model without bearing in mind that their Low tiers are unreliable, as is the privileges required component of CVSS vectors.
A CVE should be checked before a predicted 9.8 score moves it to the front of the triage queue.
