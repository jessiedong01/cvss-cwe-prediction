\section{Conclusion}
A 3.8B-parameter model fine-tuned for \nLoraTrainHours{} hours predicts every NVD enrichment field better than strong TF-IDF baselines on a full year of later CVEs.
The margins are modest, and TF-IDF remains a fast, strong baseline that any new method should beat.
Computing the score from the predicted vector keeps outputs consistent and helps a linear model, but it shifts errors toward over-calling \textsc{Critical}, a trade-off that users should choose deliberately.
The rarest severity tier, \textsc{Low}, is still poorly predicted by every system.
The next steps are rebalancing the training data, a second training epoch, larger open models, and CVSS v4.0.
