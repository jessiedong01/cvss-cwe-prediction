\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{CVSS vector prediction.}
\citet{elbaz2020fighting} predicted each CVSS metric from a bag-of-words description with a linear model and derived the score from the predicted vector, with an emphasis on decisions that an analyst could inspect at disclosure time.
CVSS-BERT~\citep{shahid2021cvssbert} trained one BERT classifier per CVSS v3.1 metric and computed the score with the equations of the specification.
\citet{marchiori2025canllms} compared general-purpose LLMs with embedding-based classifiers on the same task and found the classifiers stronger on the three impact metrics.
We keep the predict-the-vector design of these works and add a fine-tuned small language model, a strict temporal split, and a direct comparison against score regression.

\paragraph{Severity and CWE classification.}
VLAI~\citep{bonhomme2025vlai} fine-tunes RoBERTa on more than 600,000 vulnerabilities to predict the severity tier.
Its training set spans many years and includes 2024, so there is no fair way to score it on our test set (Section~\ref{sec:data}).
For CWE assignment, \citet{aota2020automation} used bag-of-words features with random forests, and \citet{mosievskiy2026roberta} reports that a 125M-parameter RoBERTa competes with much larger LLMs.

\paragraph{LLMs for vulnerability triage.}
\citet{talibzade2025llms} found that TF-IDF classifiers beat general-purpose LLMs with generic prompts on CWE and severity assignment and attributed this to the schematic wording of CVE descriptions.
Our zero-shot results agree with theirs.
CVE-LLM~\citep{ghosh2025cvellm} fine-tunes an LLM with a domain ontology to evaluate medical-device vulnerabilities, and \citet{torkamani2025casey} use LLMs to assign CWEs and severity inside a triage tool.
On the related task of predicting exploitation, \citet{roytman2025benchmarking} reports that frontier LLMs fall well short of the statistical EPSS model and cost far more to run at scale, and industry systems such as VulnWatch~\citep{databricks2025vulnwatch} prioritize vulnerabilities with hosted LLMs.
Our setting differs from these systems in two ways: everything runs on local hardware, and every output is scored on vulnerabilities disclosed after the training data.
